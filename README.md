# Caffeine

### Caffeinate your mac

Caffeine is yet another caffeination application for macos.
Unlike most other caffeinators, this one is harder to activate and deactive, you have to left click the icon in your menu bar to get access to the toggle, this makes it so that you dont ever accidentally turn the caffeination on or off.

---
### Features

There are prebuilt options from 15 minutes to 8 hours, you can alternatively also set the a custom duration from 1 minute to 24 hours.
You can configure tha app to keep on working after your battery precentage goes below 20% , by default it is configured to **not** do this in order to save power.

**Note :** all settings and selected time duration will be reset to their defaults uppon releaunch , this is because the app is meant to be disposable and temporary.
